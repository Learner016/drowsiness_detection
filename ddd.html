OpenCV.js measures brightness and focus; low-quality camera frames pause the alert timer. Face landmarks use MediaPipe’s web face-landmarker model, which is substantially more reliable for eye geometry than a basic Haar cascade. This is a demonstration, not a vehicle safety device.
